# Cleaning Profiles

## Purpose

Cleaning Profiles define the type of cleaning required for a property.

A Cleaning Profile determines:

* expected work
* estimated duration
* compensation calculation
* checklist variant

The property defines the work.

The cleaner defines the cost of performing that work.

---

# Standard Profile

## Purpose

Default turnover cleaning.

Used for:

* most reservations
* standard guest departures

### Estimated Duration

Example:

3.0 hours

### Includes

* full checklist
* laundry handling
* property preparation
* consumable replenishment
* final inspection

---

# Light Profile

## Purpose

Reduced cleaning workload.

Typical examples:

* short stays
* small number of guests
* property already in excellent condition

### Estimated Duration

Example:

2.0 hours

### Includes

* standard cleaning
* reduced laundry workload
* reduced reset effort

### Notes

Selection may be automatic or manual.

Owner may upgrade to Standard.

---

# Deep Clean Profile

## Purpose

Enhanced cleaning.

Typical examples:

* long stays
* end-of-season cleaning
* issue reported
* owner request

### Estimated Duration

Example:

5.0 hours

### Includes

* full standard cleaning
* detailed cleaning tasks
* additional inspection

---

# Profile Selection

V1:

Profile selected manually by owner/admin.

Default:

Standard Profile.

Future versions may recommend profiles based on:

* stay duration
* number of guests
* previous guest reviews
* season
* property condition

---

# Pricing Philosophy

Mission compensation is calculated using:

Estimated Duration
× Cleaner Hourly Rate

plus

* travel compensation
* urgency bonus
* future adjustments

Example:

Property:
Light Clean
2.0 hours

Cleaner:
16 €/hour

Travel:
8 €

Urgency:
6 €

Total:
46 €
